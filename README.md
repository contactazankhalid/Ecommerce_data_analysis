# Ecommerce_data_analysis
# OverviewThis 
project provides an in-depth analysis of e-commerce business operations, evaluating category financial metrics, regional sales distribution, customer engagement, and delivery performance. The primary goal is to identify revenue drivers, operational bottlenecks, and actionable business strategies. 
 # Key Highlights & MetricsTop Revenue Generator:
 Electronics leads overall sales with $1.83M Net Revenue across 1,777 orders.
 Regional Distribution: Sales are evenly balanced across all four regions, with the West region generating the highest revenue ($1.35M).  Operational Bottleneck: Delivery times stretch up to 10 days, leading to a severe ratio of late vs. fast deliveries (11:1) and negatively impacting customer ratings.  
 # Ratings & Operational FindingsCustomer Ratings: 
 Average category ratings remain low across the board (~2.94 to 3.01). 
 Fulfillment Issues: Order delivery speed directly correlates with lower satisfaction scores.  
# Strategic RecommendationsLogistics Optimization: 
Re-engineer the supply chain to cut delivery fulfillment windows from 6–10 days down to 2–3 days. 
Targeted Marketing: Increase promotional campaigns for Home and Beauty segments to drive higher volume. 
Discount Management: Recalibrate steep category discounts (up to ~18.4%) to protect net operating margins.
